## About

**Diphthong Dash** is a progressive web app (PWA) designed for elementary students (K-3) learning to read. It teaches vowel-team patterns (diphthongs) and sight words through three engaging game modes:

- **DASH** — Fast-paced, timed challenges (competitive learning)
- **LEARNING** — Untimed, guided exploration with audio support
- **HEART WORDS** — Sight word mastery with smart, AI-generated distractors

Built on learning science principles (spaced repetition, immediate feedback, offline-first design), Diphthong Dash transforms phonics from drill-and-kill worksheets into an engaging experience kids ask to play.

**Target Users:** 
- Parents of struggling readers (K-3)
- Classroom teachers seeking supplemental tools
- Homeschooling families

## FAQs

**Q: Is Diphthong Dash free?**  
A: Yes. Completely free, no ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection.

**Q: How is it different from other phonics games?**  
A: We focus specifically on vowel teams (diphthongs), which unlock 40%+ of reading. Our algorithm uses spaced repetition — we show patterns right when students are about to forget them.

**Q: Does it work on iPads/tablets?**  
A: Yes. Diphthong Dash is a PWA (progressive web app) that works on any device with a browser. Add to home screen for app-like experience.

**Q: Can teachers use it in classrooms?**  
A: Yes. Teachers can assign to individual students or use during phonics instruction. Free for classrooms. No classroom setup required.

**Q: What if a student struggles with a pattern?**  
A: The app detects struggling patterns and shows them more frequently (spaced repetition). Teachers can see which patterns each student needs more work on.

**Q: Is there a subscription option?**  
A: No. Diphthong Dash is completely free. We believe learning tools should be accessible to everyone.

**Q: What about data privacy?**  
A: No data collection. Progress is stored locally on the device. We don't track, sell, or share any user information.
