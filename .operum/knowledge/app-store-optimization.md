## 4. Icon & Visual Branding

### App Icon (Required: 1024x1024)

**Design Brief:**
- Game controller emoji (🎮) as primary visual
- "Diphthong Dash" wordmark
- Bold, playful colors (blue, green, pink)
- Clear at 180x180px (App Store thumbnail size)
- Professional but approachable

**Design Iterations to Test:**
1. Game controller + wordmark (clean, minimal)
2. Game controller + character (playful)
3. Controller + vowel team letters (educational)
4. Abstract phonics concept (modern, abstract)

**Testing:** A/B test 2 icon variations in soft launch to see which drives higher click-through.

### Screenshots: Design Principles

- **Clarity:** Text large and readable
- **Action:** Show gameplay, not static screens
- **Progress:** Visual progress bars, celebrations
- **Emotion:** Kids smiling, parents proud
- **Text:** Benefit-focused copy, not feature description
- **Consistency:** Same colors, fonts, style across all 8 images

## 6. Reviews & Ratings Strategy

### Post-Launch (Week 1-2)
- Target: 10+ reviews, 4.5+ rating
- Action: Email beta testers asking for reviews
- Incentive: Feature their child's achievement in next update (no payment for reviews - against app store rules)

### Month 1
- Target: 30+ reviews, maintain 4.5+ rating
- Monitor reviews for feedback
- Address 1-star reviews (usually have fixable issues)
- Respond to all reviews professionally

### Month 2-3
- Target: 100+ reviews, maintain 4.5+ rating
- Implement feature requests from reviews
- Highlight positive reviews in marketing

### Addressing Negative Reviews

**If review says:** "Game is too hard"
Response: "Thank you for feedback! Can you tell us which patterns your child is struggling with? We'd like to help personalize difficulty."

**If review says:** "Lots of ads"
Response: "We don't show ads — everything is free with no distractions. Can you tell us what you're seeing? We'd like to fix this."

**If review says:** "Doesn't work on my device"
Response: "We're sorry you're having trouble. Please email us at [email] with your device and iOS version. We want to help."

## 7. Download Conversion Funnel

### Metrics to Track

**App Store Page:**
- Impressions (how many see it)
- Product page views (how many click through)
- Conversion rate: Views → Download (target: 30%+)

**Post-Install:**
- Installation success rate (target: 95%+)
- Launch rate (% who open after installing)
- Day 1 retention (% still using after 1 day)
- Week 1 retention (% still using after 7 days)
- Month 1 retention (% still using after 30 days)

### Optimization Levers

**If conversion rate is low (<20%):**
- Test different icon (A/B test 2 versions)
- Rewrite description (emphasize different benefit)
- Reorder screenshots (show success/progress first)
- Update preview video

**If launch rate is low (<50%):**
- Improve onboarding (first-run experience)
- Add tutorial for new users
- Simplify initial game mode selection

**If retention is low (<30% week 1):**
- Difficulty may be too hard (first level should be winnable)
- Onboarding may be confusing
- Push notifications may help (optional, respectful)
